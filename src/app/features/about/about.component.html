Our Assam Roots

Born in the Misty Valleys of Upper Assam

For generations, the fertile soil along the Brahmaputra river valley has produced the world’s most sought-after tea leaves — celebrated for their deep maltiness, rich amber liquor, and invigorating aroma.

However, most premium teas were marked up heavily for luxury markets, leaving daily Indian households with low-grade adulterated dust. **EveryDay Tea** was established to break this barrier — connecting smallholder Assam tea estates directly to your morning chai cup.
