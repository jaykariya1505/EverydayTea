Trace the 5-step journey of EveryDay Tea from fresh leaves in Assam to the piping hot cup in your hands.

1

Nurtured in the nutrient-rich soils of Upper Assam along the Brahmaputra river valley.

2

Hand-Picked Leaves

Master tea pluckers hand-select tender tea leaves & buds at peak morning freshness.

3

Natural Processing

Withered, rolled, and oxidized under expert thermal control to lock in natural leaf strength.

4

Every batch undergoes cupping tests for deep amber color, rich maltiness, and aroma.

5

Freshly Delivered

Vacuum-sealed at the estate source and shipped straight to your nearest kitchen pantry.
